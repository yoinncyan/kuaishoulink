# Project Instructions

1. Before planning or editing this project, read `PROJECT_MEMORY.md`.
2. Treat `PROJECT_MEMORY.md` as the durable cross-session record for product goals, confirmed decisions, current progress, and next steps.
3. Update `PROJECT_MEMORY.md` whenever a major architectural decision is confirmed or a development phase is completed.
4. Do not replace confirmed decisions silently. Record the change and its reason in the decision log.
